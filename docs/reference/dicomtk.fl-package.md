# dicomtk.fl: Federated Learning, Trusted Third-Party Custody, and Differential Privacy for 'DICOM'

Federated learning and trusted third-party ('TTP') custody satellite for
'dicomtk'. Provides 'Mainzelliste' REST client integration with offline
fallback, polymorphic encryption and pseudonymisation ('PEP') for
cross-project unlinkability, hardened Bloom-filter privacy-preserving
record linkage ('PPRL') with randomized response bit-flipping and
balanced padding, (epsilon, delta)-differential privacy ('DP') federated
statistics with a formal privacy budget accountant and minimum-cell
k-anonymity suppression, site-package exporters for 'Flower', 'NVIDIA
FLARE', and 'OpenFL', and cryptographic 'site_admin' outbound approval
gating.
